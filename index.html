Research

      
My M.Tech dissertation and the research study behind my intrusion detection work.

    


    

      

        

          M.Tech dissertation
          v1.3 · live console
          Citable, Zenodo DOI
        

        
CP-DFIR: A Scalable Cross-Platform DFIR Framework for Automated Evidence Collection, Correlation and Cloud Forensic Investigation

        
When a system is breached, investigators lose hours collecting logs and artifacts by hand from different machines. CP-DFIR collects evidence from Windows, Linux and AWS automatically, converts it to one common schema and correlates it into a single attack timeline using 57 MITRE ATT&CK-mapped rules.

        
Every step is written to a tamper-evident, HMAC-signed chain-of-custody ledger. In testing it detected 25 out of 25 simulated evidence-tampering attempts.
